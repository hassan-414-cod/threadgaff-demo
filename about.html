Our Vision

A white-label route into quality clothing, without building the supply chain yourself

Threadgaff exists to give businesses and organisations a white-label route into quality clothing, without having to build their own supply chain, design capability or manufacturing relationships from scratch. A client picks from our product range, works with us on the design, and we handle sourcing, production and delivery under their name — not ours.

“To be the white-label partner that lets any brand, retailer or organiser launch quality clothing under their own name, without building a supply chain of their own.”

Our current focus is entirely B2B. B2C is on hold for later — everything on this site is built around business customers placing orders, not individual shoppers.

Production first, storytelling second

Threadgaff was built around one idea: that the tool for configuring a garment and the factory that produces it should be the same company. What you build in the Designer isn't a mockup for someone else to reinterpret — it's the spec our own floor works from.

Where production happens

Our core cutting, stitching and finishing operations run out of Pakistan. It's where our pattern-making and quality-control teams are based, and where the majority of every order is built.

For orders that need it, partner capacity extends beyond our own floor — the same specification and the same quality checks apply regardless of which facility runs the job.

How we hold quality

  • Sample approval before bulk production

    No order enters full production until a physical sample matches the approved digital proof.

  • In-line inspection during production

    Units are checked at cutting, stitching and finishing, not only at the end of the run.

  • Final inspection before shipping

    Every completed order is checked against the original spec sheet before it's packed.

Certifications and compliance documentation are available on request and will be listed here once confirmed for this build.
